How to read these numbers

Sales under $10,000 are excluded from both the median price and the sales count. Sales at $10,000 or more are included, with no upper price limit.

The median is the middle sale price after sorting; for an even number of sales, it is the average of the two middle prices. Prices are rounded to whole dollars. Each row in the dataset counts as one sale; repeated addresses are retained.

These figures describe the sales in this dataset, not all sales in each city. Values are reported by the source and have not been verified against county records. Dates, property types, and physical characteristics are not used to filter these figures.
